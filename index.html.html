Decision-makersWho signs off?InfluencersWho shapes their view?CoachesWho tells you how things really work?

MMSThe CRM: track the opportunity, its stage and approvals.MMPPrice the deal: staffing, rates and margin.SOWScope, deliverables, assumptions, client obligations.MMESet up the contract, then forecast and track in delivery.

CCI / CMContribution margin: what the deal earns after costs.EACEstimate at completion: forecast total cost and revenue.NBMNew Business Management: deal approval.WBSThe code people charge time and costs to.

  • Margin dropping without a forecast update
